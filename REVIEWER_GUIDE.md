# Kwideo Reviewer Guide

Kwideo is a feature-focused video editor for Mac, with the iPad edition coming soon. It is designed around editing capability rather than a bundled stock-content or template library.

**Available now for Mac. Coming soon to iPad. One purchase covers both platforms.**

[**View Kwideo on the Mac App Store →**](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kwideo-pro-video-editor/id6805311541?mt=12)

This repository contains product captures and documentation only; the commercial application source remains private.

1. Watch the [macOS preview](media/macos/kwideo-macos-preview.mp4).
2. Inspect the macOS captures for the workflows most relevant to you.

## Editorial angle

Kwideo is best evaluated as an editor for people who already have footage and assets and care more about the breadth and quality of editing workflows than a bundled content catalog.

The repository is intentionally evidence-first: the product captures are here so claims can be checked against the interface rather than repeated from marketing copy.
