# Kwideo Reviewer Guide

Kwideo is a feature-focused video editor for iPad and macOS. It is designed around editing capability rather than a bundled stock-content or template library.

This repository contains product captures only; the commercial application source remains private.

## Fast evaluation path

1. Watch the [iPad preview](media/ipad/kwideo-ipad-preview.mp4).
2. Watch the [macOS preview](media/macos/kwideo-macos-preview.mp4).
3. Inspect the workflow captures most relevant to your audience.

## Editorial angle

Kwideo is best evaluated as an editor for people who already have footage and assets and care more about the breadth and quality of editing workflows than a bundled content catalog.

The repository is intentionally evidence-first: the product captures are here so claims can be checked against the interface rather than repeated from marketing copy.
